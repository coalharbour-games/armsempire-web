What we collect

Nothing. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no forms. Fonts and images are served from this website; nothing is loaded from third parties.

When the homepage shows an announcement, your browser remembers that you have seen it until you close the browser (session storage), so it does not appear again. This stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. To deliver the pages and protect them from attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type and keeps it briefly in server logs. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Links to other services

Links to Discord, YouTube, X, Bluesky, Instagram and LinkedIn take you to those services. Once you are there, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in the Czech Republic that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz). Write to us at the address above.
