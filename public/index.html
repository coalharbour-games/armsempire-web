Operations

How the business works

01

Design

Research unlocks parts, not finished weapons. Pick the frame, barrel, action, caliber and feed. The blueprint and the unit cost update as you build.

02

Supply

You don’t own the mines. Buy coal, iron ore, copper and sulfur from companies on the map, and hope the border stays open.

03

Produce

Build factories near raw materials or near your buyers. Expand into research centers and testing grounds. An idle factory burns cash.

04

Sell

Democracies, monarchies and military juntas send offers. Win tenders against rival corporations, or take the deal nobody else will.

05

Lobby

Meet ministers, fund parties, time the elections. Covert support is cheaper. Until somebody finds out.

06

Live with it

The world keeps a record of where your products ended up. Reputation, embargoes and headlines follow.

The world

Every state
has a price.

The map is generated for every new game. New states, new governments, new people in charge. Find out who is buying, who is about to be, and what it costs to stay friends.

  1. 1

    Generated states

    A new set of countries every game. Democracies, monarchies, theocracies, juntas.

  2. 2

    The people in charge

    Ministers with traits, stability and an opinion of you.

  3. 3

    Priority contracts

    Good relations open doors. And order books.

  4. 4

    Transparent or covert

    Fund a party in the open, or quietly. Quietly is cheaper. Until it isn’t.

  5. 5

    Factions and elections

    Governments fall. Doctrines change. Your best customer can be gone in one vote.
